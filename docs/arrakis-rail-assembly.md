## Assembly

1. Print everything. Sand the lap joints and the sleeve bore lightly; test-slide 3 in 1: it should move with light hand pressure (0.5 mm clearance).
2. Head: press the four M4 inserts into 11. Stick the strips into the channels of 10, wire them in parallel in the trough, pass the cable through the passage and the grommet, zip-tie it, snap the cover 12 in.
3. Platform: fit 14 into the landing on the front dune (nut from inside), 15 into the rear wall. Solder 14 into the + lead; lay the leads in the platform cavity. Press the pads 8 into the corner recesses.
4. Glue 2 onto 1 at the 170 lap joint (CA or epoxy); the teardrop profile sets the orientation. Glue 4 onto 3 at the 190 joint and sand the outside seam flush: it is the sliding surface.
5. Pass the head cable down the tube from the slot. Slide the tube into the sleeve, crest to crest. Lift it fully, glue the key 7 into its pocket through the port at 232, and close the port with the plug.
6. Drop the shoe 6 into the boss, cradle toward the tube, and screw the knob 5 in until it bites. Half a turn out frees the tube.
7. Clamp the head: put 10 and 11 around the tube with the cable through the slot and the passage, collar bottom 256 to 316 up the tube, and tighten the four screws 13 evenly to a 1 mm split gap.
8. Connect the cable to the button and the inlet inside the platform, snap the plate 9 in, plug in 12 V, and test the lock at the lowest and highest head position.

## Checks after assembly

- The tube slides through its full 130 mm with the knob half a turn out, and does not move with the knob tight and 2 kg hung on the blade tip.
- The head cable has slack inside the sleeve at the highest position and does not kink at the lowest.
- All three pads touch; the lamp does not rock when the blade tip is pressed.
- Button cycles the strips; the XT60E-M polarity matches the supply (centre pin convention of your supply, check before the first power-up).
