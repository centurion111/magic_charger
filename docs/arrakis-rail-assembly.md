# Arrakis Rail — assembly guide (Seeker pole, Shield Wall foot, Hawk head)

Companion to the Claude Design canvas sheets 11 (general view), 12 (assembly guide) and 13 (the bisected-pyramid foot), Rev A, 2026-10-22. Part numbers match the balloons on sheet 12. Dimensions in mm; the frames are those of the two CAD briefs.

## The foot

A rounded-corner pyramid, base 235 × 260 (X −70 … 165, Y ±130, corners R25), virtual apex at (0, 0, 175), cut through along the arm's axis by a 50 mm slot (|Y| < 25). The Seeker sleeve stands in the cleft, merged with both halves; the halves top out at 142 where they meet it. Faces 46° (front), 53° (sides), 68° (back); 5 mm walls, open below, four TPU pads at (140, ±105) and (−45, ±105). Button on the right half's front face at (100, 50, 70), axis normal to the face; XT60E-M on the left half's back face at (−63, −60, 20). Ø10 wiring holes through the sleeve wall at Z = 30 into each half.

## Assembly

1. Print everything. Sand the lap joints and the sleeve bore lightly; test-slide 3 in 1: it should move with light hand pressure (0.5 mm clearance).
2. Head: press the four M4 inserts into 11. Stick the strips into the channels of 10, wire them in parallel in the trough, pass the cable through the passage and the grommet, zip-tie it, snap the cover 12 in.
3. Foot: fit 14 into the landing on the right half's front face (nut from inside), 15 into the left half's back face. Solder 14 into the + lead; the leads run through the Ø10 holes in the sleeve at z = 30. Press the four pads 8 into the corner recesses.
4. If the foot was printed as two halves, glue the right half onto the sleeve's bulge and the left half's base plate first. Glue 2 onto 1 at the 170 lap joint (CA or epoxy); the teardrop profile sets the orientation. Glue 4 onto 3 at the 190 joint and sand the outside seam flush: it is the sliding surface.
5. Pass the head cable down the tube from the slot. Slide the tube into the sleeve, crest to crest. Lift it fully, glue the key 7 into its pocket through the port at 232, and close the port with the plug.
6. Drop the shoe 6 into the boss, cradle toward the tube, and screw the knob 5 in until it bites. Half a turn out frees the tube.
7. Clamp the head: put 10 and 11 around the tube with the cable through the slot and the passage, collar bottom 256 to 316 up the tube, and tighten the four screws 13 evenly to a 1 mm split gap.
8. Connect the cable to the button and the inlet inside the halves, snap the floor plates 9 in, plug in 12 V, and test the lock at the lowest and highest head position.

## Checks after assembly

- The tube slides through its full 130 mm with the knob half a turn out, and does not move with the knob tight and 2 kg hung on the blade tip.
- The head cable has slack inside the sleeve at the highest position and does not kink at the lowest.
- All four pads touch; the lamp does not rock when the blade tip is pressed.
- Button cycles the strips; the XT60E-M polarity matches the supply (check before the first power-up).
